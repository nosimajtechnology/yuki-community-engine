# Community remix boundaries

## Safe remix space

- New poses, expressions, props, activities, environments, camera angles, and moods.
- Period-grounded everyday settings and clearly labeled retro-futurist scenes.
- Alternate outfits when labeled as alternates and Yuki's face, hair, wing ornaments, proportions, and palette anchors stay recognizable.
- Fictional products, commercials, jokes, and episodes clearly presented as community interpretations.
- Cross-community concepts when permissions and attribution are clear.

## Requires team/canon review

- Permanent redesigns, new signature accessories, alternate hair, powers, biography, relationships, origin stories, or named Y2Kverse institutions.
- Official-looking claims, campaign promises, token utility, endorsements, or merchandise.
- Replacing the canonical turnaround or changing reference priority.

## Do not do

- Present generated art or fan lore as official canon.
- Remove attribution or imply ownership of Yuki/Y2K IP.
- Use hateful, exploitative, deceptive, or non-consensual contexts.
- Sexualize the character; the canonical sheet establishes a youthful stylized presentation and no exact age.
- Copy an unrelated character's protected identity and call it Yuki.
- Add a public license, release, or distribution channel without approval.

## Labeling

Recommended labels: `canonical study`, `official-source grounded`, `community convention`, `historically grounded`, `era-plausible composite`, and `creative interpretation`.
