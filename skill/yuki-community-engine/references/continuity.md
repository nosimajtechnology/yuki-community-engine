# Continuity and Repair

## Project lock

After a visual is approved, retain:

- selected style adapter and version, style-local expression preset, motion
  profile, and assigned identity/style/project/motion reference roles
- any transformation pre-state, change-only delta, and post-state
- Yuki's exact face, eyes, hair shape, hair length, ornaments, body proportions,
  glove scale, boot scale, and current expression
- current wardrobe, wear, accessories, and carried objects
- supporting characters and their designs
- prop/vehicle silhouette, color, side, orientation, and damage state
- environment layout, entrances, exits, furniture, landmarks, and horizon
- time, weather, light direction, palette, and rendering contract
- selected rendering-reference set and each reference's assigned role
- approved geometry budget, texture density, material/lighting model, effects
  density, draw distance, and capture characteristics
- camera side, screen direction, action phase, and unresolved motion

## Progressive action

Every shot must imply what happened immediately before, what is happening now,
and what the next cut can reveal. Move the action forward. Do not replay the same
beat from several angles unless the repetition is an intentional impact device.

For episode boards, use the final approved panel of the prior board as the next
board's start-state authority. Restate the continuation lock internally before
generating.

## Orientation audit

Before presenting a storyboard, verify:

1. left/right positions remain coherent
2. eyelines and travel direction match geography
3. held objects remain in the correct hand or side
4. entrances, doors, vehicles, and landmarks do not jump
5. light and weather do not reset
6. clothing and damage states progress rather than revert
7. the last panel creates a viable next action state
8. later panels do not gain polygons, PBR materials, denser effects, upgraded
   lighting, remaster sharpness, or modern post-processing
9. a registered adapter does not drift toward modern digital anime, promotional
   art, another period style, or the flagship PS2 build

## Narrow repair

Respond with:

```text
PROBLEM
[one short diagnosis]

FIX
[minimum important correction]

RETRY
[copy-paste correction or corrected generation instruction]
```

Use:

```text
LOCK:
[all approved layers]

CHANGE ONLY:
[failed layer]

DO NOT CHANGE:
[identity, world, rendering, composition, chronology, and other correct layers]
```

## Priority order

1. wrong Yuki identity or generic anime substitution
2. hair color, length, mass, or bangs drift
3. missing or mutated wing ornaments
4. age/body-proportion drift or sexualization
5. extra/missing limbs, fingers, gloves, or boots
6. face or eye drift
7. wardrobe/logo mutation
8. rendering-era drift from the approved screenshot-derived contract
9. registered style-adapter drift
10. geography, screen direction, or action reset
11. prop/vehicle mutation
12. decorative errors

Repair one panel when one panel failed. Regenerate the full frame or board only
when several protected layers conflict or the authority itself is unusable.
