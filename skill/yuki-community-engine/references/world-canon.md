# Y2Kverse World Layer

## Evidence classes

Keep these separate.

### Verified project signals

- Dotcom presents itself around **nostalgia culture** and **the Renaissance**.
- Yuki / YUKI.EXE is presented as a Y2Kverse character and a 2000s kid.
- Official/community-facing campaigns encourage original, dynamic Yuki edits and
  remix participation.

These signals define tone, not a complete fictional biography. Do not invent
family, origin mechanics, powers, relationships, or plot history as official.

### Community conventions

Public community material frequently places Yuki inside recognizable 2000s game,
summer, internet, and nostalgia scenarios. Treat recurring treatments as remix
language unless an official source explicitly canonizes them.

### Creative interpretation

New locations, jobs, adventures, products, antagonists, and episode plots are
allowed. Label them as scene concepts or community-created lore, not verified
canon.

## World range

Use a broad 1998-2005 vocabulary when appropriate:

- CRT monitors, beige towers, translucent electronics, wired mice
- dial-up screens, instant messenger windows, personal homepages, web portals
- cyber cafés, school computer labs, mall arcades, video rental stores
- CD players, jewel cases, flip phones, pagers, MiniDisc players
- compact digital cameras, camcorders, photo kiosks
- bedrooms, food courts, convenience stores, parking garages, beach summers
- anime/game retail, demo discs, magazine ads, desktop wallpapers
- optimistic millennium futurism alongside ordinary suburban life

Do not collapse the world into generic chrome, bubble typography, cyberpunk neon,
or a mood-board collage. Most scenes should contain one to three readable period
signals inside a believable place.

## Historical grounding

When a recognizable real product, interface, game platform, business, or place
matters:

1. research authentic 1998-2005 references first
2. separate real historical details from Y2Kverse invention
3. ground geometry, materials, typography, screen ratios, and object scale
4. avoid copying protected logos or characters unless the user explicitly asks
   and the intended use is appropriate

Use a fictionalized period-equivalent when exact branding is unnecessary.

## Rendering freedom

Yuki's canon is model and style independent. She may appear as:

- clean canonical 3D character art
- early-2000s console or PC game graphics
- anime cel, magazine illustration, web graphic, or toy photography
- modern cinematic media

Do not force PS1/PS2 fidelity unless requested or appropriate. When a specific
game-era look is requested, build the assets at that era's actual geometry,
texture, lighting, UI, and camera level rather than applying a surface filter.

## Tone

Favor sincere nostalgia, playful internet strangeness, ordinary life, adventure,
and community remixability. Absurd scenes work best when the world treats them as
normal. Avoid unrequested financial hype.
