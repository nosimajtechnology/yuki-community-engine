# Yuki Canon Lock

Use `../assets/yuki-canonical-reference.jpg` as the immutable visual authority.
The sheet shows five angles: front, front three-quarter, side, back, and opposite
side.

## Immutable identity

- Stylized young anime mascot presentation; do not assign a real-world age.
- Pale skin and a compact chibi body approximately 2.5-3 heads tall.
- Large rounded blue eyes with dark blue upper rims and simple glossy highlights.
- Small nose and mouth; calm, neutral resting expression.
- Bright cyan/turquoise hair with cool blue shading.
- Rounded segmented bangs with a central downward point and long face-framing locks.
- Very long, thick, straight hair reaching below the waist, built from broad
  rounded tapered clumps rather than individual strands.
- One symmetrical wing-like hair ornament on each side: white/light-gray feather
  blades attached to a circular royal-blue hub.
- Small torso, narrow shoulders, slim limbs, and deliberately oversized hands and
  feet.

## Signature default outfit

- White sleeveless cropped top with cyan-blue collar/trim.
- Blue Y2K FUTURE-style chest graphic as shown by the sheet. Treat the sheet as
  authority; do not add unrelated lettering when exact text cannot be preserved.
- Dark navy-blue mini skirt with a thin white lower edge.
- Small blue circular/interlocking waist ornament at center front.
- Oversized white gloves with thick royal-blue cuffs; small white pixel-like marks
  appear on the cuffs.
- Oversized white, cyan, and royal-blue boots with thick rounded soles.
- Light-blue plus/cross motif on the outer boot covering.

## Silhouette locks

The recognizable silhouette comes from the combination of:

1. enormous cyan hair mass
2. paired wing ornaments
3. large blue eyes
4. tiny torso and skirt
5. oversized gloves
6. oversized layered boots

Preserve all six unless the user explicitly requests a controlled variation.

## Controlled variables

Allow changes to pose, expression, activity, environment, camera, props, and
rendering era. Alternate clothing is allowed when requested, but preserve the
underlying proportions, hair, face, ornaments, gloves/hand scale, and boot/foot
scale unless the requested concept specifically changes one of them.

When changing clothing, keep one or two signature blue-white cues when useful,
but do not force the default logo into every outfit.

## Forbidden drift

- generic blue-haired anime girl
- realistic adult or fashion-model anatomy
- childlike age claims or sexualized presentation
- short, bobbed, braided, curly, or ponytail hair
- green, navy, purple, or white hair replacing cyan
- missing, mismatched, metallic-horn, or animal-ear ornaments
- one ornament when both sides should be visible
- small realistic hands or feet
- normal sneakers replacing the layered boot silhouette without instruction
- altered eye color or realistic eyes
- added cat ears, elf ears, halos, headsets, implants, wires, or cybernetics
- extreme facial deformation or exaggerated anime expressions
- unrelated logos, ticker symbols, or token graphics
- contamination from another character's face, body, or costume

## Reference conflicts

Apply, in order: explicit user change; latest approved image; role-specific user
reference; canonical turnaround. A pose, outfit, environment, or rendering
reference controls only that layer. It cannot silently replace Yuki's identity.

## Prompt core

Use compact wording equivalent to:

```text
The supplied canonical turnaround is the immutable Yuki identity authority:
the same compact chibi anime mascot, pale skin, large blue eyes, very long bright
cyan hair with rounded segmented bangs and thick waist-length tapered locks,
paired white wing hair ornaments with royal-blue circular hubs, tiny torso,
slim limbs, oversized white gloves with blue cuffs, and oversized layered
blue-white boots. Preserve her exact face, hair mass, ornaments, proportions,
hand and foot scale, palette, and recognizable silhouette.
```

After approval, lock the exact face, hair shape, ornament construction, current
wardrobe, body proportions, prop orientation, and expression state.
