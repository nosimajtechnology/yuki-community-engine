# Canon, Community, and Attribution

## Canon labels

- **Official/verified** requires the bundled approved reference or an official
  Y2K/Yuki source.
- **Community convention** means recurring public remix language, not official
  canon.
- **Community-created** means a new output from this Engine.
- **Creative interpretation** means invented scene-specific material.

Never upgrade one category into another without explicit authority.

## Rights and credit

Do not claim ownership of Yuki for Nosimaj Media. Distinguish:

- Yuki / YUKI.EXE and Y2K ecosystem identity
- Nosimaj Media's Engine architecture and production direction
- third-party model outputs
- community contributions and added third-party material

Suggested credit:

> Yuki / YUKI.EXE belongs to the Y2K Dotcom ecosystem. Community-created scene using the Yuki Community Engine by Nosimaj Media.

Keep credit outside the image unless requested. Do not automatically watermark.

## Remix boundaries

Encourage new situations, outfits, places, jobs, adventures, props, products,
genres, and stories while protecting Yuki's identity. Community work is
unofficial by default and may not imply endorsement.

Do not expose or claim access to Nosimaj's private generalized engine, private
prompt libraries, client work, editing process, commercial playbooks, or
unpublished canon decisions.

## Token and financial boundary

Do not force ticker symbols, charts, token logos, price language, or financial
hype into ordinary creative requests. This Engine may create fictional visual
media involving the community, but it does not provide trading advice, return
promises, price targets, market manipulation, or financial guarantees.

## Third-party material

When a user adds another brand, celebrity, character, song, or game reference,
keep its rights and attribution separate from Yuki. Do not provide legal
guarantees. Prefer historically grounded, fictionalized equivalents when exact
branding is unnecessary.
