# Portability notes — founding external engine

## Reusable base modules

- Five-tier reference authority and conflict policy
- Model-neutral creative brief contract
- Seven-mode router and approval gates
- Continuity ledger and shot handoff rules
- Five-to-seven-shot storyboard contract
- Four-board episode arc with optional justified fifth board
- Historical-grounding protocol
- Provider adapter boundary
- Community quickstart/remix safety layer
- Deterministic repository validation

## Yuki-specific modules

- Tier 1 five-view turnaround
- Hair, face, eye, ornament, proportion, outfit, palette, and silhouette locks
- Forbidden mutations specific to blue-haired anime-character contamination
- Youthful-presentation safety boundary
- Y2K Dotcom/YUKI.EXE research register and access limitations
- Y2Kverse nostalgia vocabulary and range rules

## World-specific modules

- Nostalgia Culture / Renaissance positioning
- 1998–2005 consumer-computing and public-space vocabulary
- Distinction between historical reconstruction and millennium retro-futurism
- Community remix/contest convention

## Manual judgment that remains

- Resolving conflicting canonical references
- Deciding whether an alternate outfit still reads as Yuki
- Determining whether a fifth episode board is structurally justified
- Identifying when real historical research is material to fidelity
- Simplifying a concept without losing its hook
- Reviewing model output for face/silhouette drift
- Confirming rights and attribution for cross-community remixes

## Automation opportunities

- Generate a first-pass canon sheet from a multi-view turnaround, then require human approval
- Compare storyboard end-state and next-board start-state ledgers
- Lint prompts for missing canon anchors and forbidden unsupported lore
- Check adapter prompts against current character limits
- Track source freshness and flag inaccessible/changed official bios
- Produce a community-safe export that omits internal-only modules

## Estimated future onboarding friction

| Step | Typical friction | Automation potential |
|---|---|---|
| Acquire approved multi-view reference | High; depends on project team | Low |
| Extract immutable traits | Medium; visual judgment | Medium |
| Verify official culture/lore | Medium-high; fragmented social sources | Medium |
| Build world vocabulary | Medium | High |
| Configure modes/continuity | Low; largely reusable | High |
| Add adapters | Low-medium; provider volatility | High |
| Validate diverse concepts | Medium; taste and failure review | Medium |

The transferable product is the authority/workflow/continuity architecture. The non-transferable bottleneck remains human canon judgment and creative direction.
