## 8. Progressive episode — EPISODE

**Input:** Yuki discovers a forgotten dial-up server still connecting to a vanished website.

**Episode spine:** A repeating connection tone leads Yuki from a public computer lab to a sealed server room, where the final connection restores one small forgotten webpage and then releases the line.

**Board plan:**

1. **Hook + Setup:** lab monitors wake simultaneously; one modem tone begins; Yuki traces the active cable to a locked floor conduit. End: access panel open, cable descends, Yuki kneels beside it.
2. **Escalation / Development:** inherit kneeling position and open panel; follow the cable through service corridors as lights activate behind her. End: Yuki reaches a sealed server-room door with the cable passing underneath.
3. **Major Development / Turn:** inherit door/cable/geography; open the room, reveal one surviving rack and a CRT showing repeated failed handshakes; Yuki reconnects one visibly loose line. End: handshake succeeds, all room lights go out except the CRT.
4. **Payoff / Resolution:** inherit darkness and successful connection; a tiny plain webpage loads, its visitor counter increments once, the modem tone resolves, and the rack powers down. End: Yuki lit by the final CRT glow; cable remains connected; no new crisis.

**Fifth board:** Not justified. Board 4 resolves the established action without overload.

**Pass:** Every board inherits the prior physical state; the website's meaning remains interpretation rather than official Y2Kverse history.
